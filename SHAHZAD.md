# SHAHZAD — Game Vision Document
*Living Creative Bible | Started August 2026*

---

## The Feeling We Are Always Chasing

Epic. Personal. Proud. A game that makes a young British-Persian man feel like the hero the world forgot to make. Cinematic at every frame. Emotionally addictive. Culturally honest. The kind of game you lose sleep over not because it's hard — but because you cannot stop feeling something.

---

## The Hero

**Name:** SHAHZAD *(Persian: Son of a King)*

A young British-Persian man living in modern London. Sharp. Warm. Confident without arrogance. He carries two worlds inside him and is only beginning to understand why. His face is his own — real, specific, unmistakable. Not a generic hero. Him.

**His duality:**
- In London he is streetwise, instinctive, modern
- In ancient Persia he is destined, powerful, mythological
- In both worlds he is the same person — that is the point

---

## The Mythology — Ahura Mazda & The Eternal War

This is not a story about good versus evil in the abstract. It is a story rooted in the oldest religion on earth — **Zoroastrianism**, the faith of ancient Persia, the faith that shaped the Achaemenid empire, the faith that predates Islam by over a thousand years.

### Ahura Mazda — The Wise Lord
The supreme deity of Zoroastrianism. Creator of all light, truth, and wisdom. His emblem is the **Faravahar** — the great winged disc with a noble Persian figure at its centre, one hand raised, the other holding the ring of divine covenant. This symbol appears carved above the portal, on SHAHZAD's armour, and into the throne at Persepolis. It was always there. Now it has meaning.

Ahura Mazda did not choose SHAHZAD at random. Three thousand years ago he sealed the boundary between the ancient world and the modern one — and he appointed one bloodline to guard it. SHAHZAD's bloodline. *Son of a King* is not just a name. It is a title Ahura Mazda gave.

### Angra Mainyu — The Destructive Spirit
The eternal opposite of Ahura Mazda. The force of darkness, chaos, and annihilation. He cannot cross into the world himself — but he can find instruments. He found one three thousand years ago. He gave a sorcerer power, immortality, and purpose: destroy the bloodline, break the seal, let darkness into both worlds permanently.

### The Villain — Ahriman's Instrument
The dark Persian sorcerer did not simply become evil. He made a covenant with Angra Mainyu — surrendering his soul in exchange for the power to outlast every generation of SHAHZAD's bloodline until he could end it. He has been waiting three thousand years. He is not angry. He is certain. That is what makes him terrifying.

### The Simorgh — Ahura Mazda's Messenger
She has watched over SHAHZAD's bloodline under Ahura Mazda's command for three thousand years. Her impatience with SHAHZAD is divine impatience — she carries the weight of every generation that came before him and is acutely aware of what happens if this one fails.

### The Sacred Script — Avestan
The Avestan script on SHAHZAD's weapons is the sacred language of Ahura Mazda's own hymns — the Gathas. When ROXANA discovers that the same script contains molecular blueprints in a vault beneath Rome, she is not finding human science. She is finding Ahura Mazda's science — knowledge encoded into the sacred language and hidden across millennia, waiting for someone of the bloodline to find it. The cure for Alzheimer's and the weapon were both placed there deliberately. By him.

### The Faravahar — The Game's Emblem
Gold. Wings open. Ancient. It appears on SHAHZAD's armour, above the portal, carved into the throne of Persepolis, and etched faintly into the brick wall of the London hideout. Wherever the bloodline has been, it left this mark. The player will recognise it before they understand it — and when they do understand it, it will mean everything.

## The Two Worlds

### Modern London
Rain-soaked streets. Neon reflections. Trafalgar Square at night. A city full of people who have no idea what moves beneath the surface. SHAHZAD's London is beautiful and gritty — blue and amber cinematic light, volumetric fog, golden embers. His hideout is beneath a Persian rug shop in East London. Ancient maps on brick walls. Modern screens glowing.

### Ancient Persia
Persepolis at its height. Towering columns. Golden hour that never ends. The sky full of mythological creatures. A world of breathtaking grandeur where SHAHZAD is not a visitor — he is royalty returning home. Deep underground palaces carved into mountains. Ancient cuneiform script on everything. Fire torches. Dust particles catching god rays.

### The Portal
A doorway of ancient Persian geometric gold that tears open in the fabric of modern London. One side: neon and rain. The other: golden Persepolis. SHAHZAD is the only one who can cross it — because Ahura Mazda sealed it to his bloodline three thousand years ago. The Faravahar glows above it every time it opens.

## The Characters

### The Simorgh
SHAHZAD's mythological guide. Enormous, ancient, magnificent — iridescent feathers of gold, crimson, turquoise and violet. Fierce wise amber eyes. The Simorgh has watched over his bloodline for three thousand years and is not entirely patient about it.

### The Villain
A dark Persian sorcerer. Ancient. Cruel. He has been waiting across centuries for SHAHZAD's bloodline to surface so he can end it permanently. Black and gold robes. A staff crowned with dark flame. He moves between worlds too — and he was there first.

### ROXANA
**Name:** ROXANA *(historically: the Persian wife of Alexander the Great — a name that carries conquest, love, and survival)*

A British-Persian scientist completing her PhD in Rome. Brilliant. Precise. She carries the same ancient blood as SHAHZAD but her gift expresses differently — not through combat, but through discovery. Where he fights to protect the ancient world, she decodes it.

**Her discovery:**
In a vault beneath Rome she finds ancient Avestan script that the academic world dismissed as ceremonial. She alone realises it is not prayer — it is molecular architecture. The same sacred Zoroastrian writing encoded into SHAHZAD's weapons contains blueprints for compounds that do not exist yet. One is the most destructive weapon ever conceived. The other is a cure for Alzheimer's disease.

**Her role in the universe:**
What ROXANA discovers in Rome, SHAHZAD protects in ancient Persia. She works in the present. He fights in the past. They are separated by millennia and connected by blood. Two games. One universe. Neither complete without the other.

**Her visual identity:**
Modern Rome setting. White lab coat over dark clothing. Ancient Avestan text glowing on screens and manuscripts around her. Her face is specific, real, unmistakable — not a scientist archetype. Her.

## The Weapons

### Ancient Persia — The Cuneiform Sword
Dark iron and bronze blade. Intricate Achaemenid engravings. Ancient Old Persian cuneiform script etched in glowing gold. No Arabic script — ever. Pre-Islamic, authentic, specific.

### Modern London — The Phaser
Matte black and dark chrome. The same Persian geometric patterns from the sword etched in gold along the barrel. Blue energy glow from the chamber. The two weapons are clearly from the same bloodline — different centuries, same soul.

## Visual Direction

**Palette:** Deep blues, amber gold, black, crimson, bronze
**Lighting:** Cinematic always. God rays in Persia. Neon reflections in London
**Quality bar:** Unreal Engine 5. Photorealistic. 8K. Anamorphic lens feel
**Reference:** AAA game art. Nothing generic. Everything earned
**Script:** Ancient Old Persian cuneiform only. Never Arabic. Never generic fantasy

- The two worlds create constant tension and wonder
- Cultural pride is baked into every frame
- The story is personal enough to feel real
